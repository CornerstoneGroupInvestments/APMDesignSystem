FIGURE 9Service Management - Key Lifecycle§9.1

1 · Procure & record

YubiKey 5C NFC FIPS (fw 5.7.4) via SoftwareOne. Serial recorded in the key register.

2 · Issue

In person where practical, otherwise tracked post to the verified address. The key ships with no credential on it.

3 · Verify & enrol

Video identity verification, serial confirmed, one-time TAP issued. Entra prompts passkey registration at sign-in.

4 · Attest & activate

Entra validates attestation and AAGUID - only a genuine FIPS YubiKey completes. TAP expires; CA enforces key-only sign-in.

5 · Operate

PIN + touch per sign-in. Eight failed PIN attempts destroys the credentials in hardware. xy holders register a spare key.

6 · Rotate / replace

Loss, damage, leaver or firmware sunset - passkey deleted in Entra within one hour of report; replacement follows steps 2–4.

7 · Revoke & destroy

Credential deletion in Entra is the authoritative revocation - the key becomes inert. Returned keys are FIDO2-reset before restocking.
