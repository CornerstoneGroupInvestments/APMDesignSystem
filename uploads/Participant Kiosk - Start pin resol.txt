           someone looks at the Start menu.
           
           Two causes worth separating before editing paths:
             - the application is genuinely not installed yet (section 2 will also show it
               missing). Install order is the fix, through the Enrolment Status Page, not a
               path change.
             - the application is installed but its shortcut sits somewhere else, or under a
               different name. Section 4 gives the real name.
