ES Device Baseline - build sheet

Duplicate the estate Windows 11 baseline, then adjust these nine lines. Every other setting stays exactly as inherited.

APM DIGITAL WORKPLACE
21 AUG 2026

The ES Participant Kiosk signs in unattended to a local account Kiosk-[SERIAL], with no participant credential and no lock screen. Nine settings in APM-W11-SEC-Baseline-P-1.2 prevent that or expose a credential prompt to a member of the public. The policy is copied rather than authored, so the remaining 585 controls cannot drift from the estate baseline.

Create the copy

  1. Intune admin centre > Devices > Windows > Configuration. Select APM-W11-SEC-Baseline-P-1.2, then … > Duplicate.
  2. Name it APM-W11-SEC-ES-Device-Baseline-P-1.0. Description: ES Participant Kiosk. Copy of APM-W11-SEC-Baseline-P-1.2 with nine settings adjusted for unattended autologon. See build sheet.
  3. Open the duplicate > Settings > Edit, and change only the nine lines below.
  4. Assignments > Include sg-dyn-dvc-cdg-participant-kiosk. Remove any group the duplicate inherited from the source policy.
  5. On APM-W11-SEC-Baseline-P-1.2 > Assignments > Exclude, add sg-dyn-dvc-cdg-participant-kiosk, so the fleet receives one baseline and not two in conflict.
